New Roots SmartStack Pitch 
Your supplements. Your schedule. Smarter timing. 
1. Executive Summary 
New Roots Herbal has an opportunity to turn its extensive supplement portfolio into a 
personalized digital experience that helps consumers understand not only what 
supplements to take, but when to take them — and why. 
New Roots SmartStack would be a mobile app that allows users to: 
• Scan a New Roots product barcode  
• Automatically identify the exact product and formulation 
• Build their personal supplement "stack" 
• Enter and schedule the number of capsules/doses they take 
• Choose their normal meal, coffee, workout and bedtime schedule 
• Automatically generate a personalized daily supplement schedule 
• Receive reminders 
• Identify potential timing conflicts between supplements 
• Identify ingredients that may compete for absorption 
• Flag relevant food/beverage interactions 
• Flag relevant medication interactions 
• Explain the reason behind each timing recommendation 
• Adjust the schedule when the user's daily routine changes 
Instead of simply saying: 
"Take your iron at 8:00 AM. " 
the app could say: 
Iron — 8:00 AM 

Take with water. 
Avoid taking at the same time as calcium. 
Your schedule has moved your calcium supplement to 12:00 PM to help separate the two. 
Coffee reminder: Your morning coffee is at 7:30 AM. We've scheduled your iron for 9:30 
AM. 
This transforms New Roots from simply being a supplement manufacturer into a company 
providing a digital supplement-management ecosystem. 
 
2. The Problem with Regular Consumers 
Consumers are increasingly taking multiple supplements at the same time. 
The problem is that supplement routines become complicated very quickly. 
Someone might take: 
• A multivitamin 
• Magnesium 
• Calcium 
• Iron 
• Vitamin D 
• Fish oil 
• Probiotics 
• Zinc 
• An herbal formula 
• A protein supplement 
• Prescription medications 
The consumer is then left to figure out: 
• What should I take with food? 
• What should I take without food? 
• What should I take in the morning? 

• What should I take at night? 
• Can I take these together? 
• Should I separate these? 
• Can I take this with coffee? 
• Can I take this with my medication? 
• Do I need to take this every day? 
• Which products already contain the same nutrients? 
• Am I accidentally doubling up on something? 
Even New Roots' own product directions can contain timing instructions. For example, the 
current Multi product says that people taking other medications should take the product a 
few hours before or after them. 
Health Canada also specifically recommends that consumers tell healthcare professionals 
about the natural health products and medications they use to prevent interactions. 
The information exists. 
The problem is that consumers don't have an easy way to organize it. 
 
3. The Solution 
New Roots SmartStack 
A personalized supplement scheduling engine. 
The consumer enters their products once. 
The system does the complicated work behind the scenes. 
Step 1 — Scan 
The consumer opens the app and scans: 
"Add Supplement" → Scan Barcode 
The system recognizes: 

New Roots Herbal 
Iron Bisglycinate 
NPN XXXXXXXX 
It automatically retrieves the product information. 
Health Canada's Licensed Natural Health Products Database contains information 
including NPN, medicinal ingredients, dosage form, recommended use and risk 
information, creating an important Canadian regulatory data source for validating product 
information. 
For New Roots products specifically, the company could maintain its own authoritative 
product database.

7. Interaction Severity System 
Not every interaction is equally important. 
The app should distinguish between: 
    Important 
Potentially significant medication/supplement interaction. 
Action required 
Do not combine without speaking with your healthcare professional. 
    Timing conflict 
Taking these together may interfere with absorption/effectiveness. 
Action recommended 
We've separated these by X hours. 
    Consideration 
Timing may be preferable but isn't necessarily critical. 
Taking this with food may be preferable. 
    Product instruction 
Directly based on the product label. 
New Roots recommends taking this product with food. 
    Informational 
General educational information. 
This prevents the app from making every interaction look like an emergency.
