# SmartStack Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [To confirm: launch date] · **Version:** 2026-10

SmartStack is a supplement-scheduling app made by **New Roots Herbal Inc.** ("New Roots Herbal",
"we"). It runs at schedule.flourishbodyandmind.com and, once installed, on your phone or
computer. This policy explains what SmartStack collects, why, where it is kept, for how long, and
the choices and rights you have. It applies to the SmartStack app only; the newrootsherbal.com
website has its own privacy policy.

We wrote SmartStack to collect as little as possible. We don't sell your information, we don't use
advertising or analytics trackers, and nothing in SmartStack uses artificial intelligence.

## 1. Using SmartStack without an account

You can use SmartStack without an account. Your routine, your stack, your bottle counts, your
shopping list and your check marks then stay **only on your device**. We don't receive them.

If you turn on **dose reminders** or **news notifications**, your device sends us the minimum
needed to deliver them:

- a random identifier created by the app (not linked to your name or email);
- your time zone, the type of device (iPhone, Android or computer) and the app's language;
- the push address your browser gives us for notifications;
- for reminders, the time of each reminder over the next 7 days and its text (by default the text
  doesn't name your products; see section 8);
- whether you turned news notifications on, and when you turned them on or off.

"Delete my data" in the app erases all of this.

## 2. With an account

An account lets you back up your stack and use it on several devices. When you create one, we
collect:

- **Account information**: your email address, your first name if you give it, your language,
  and records of your consents (which version of this policy you accepted, when, and that you
  confirmed you are 14 or older).
- **Sign-in information**: if you use a password, it never leaves your device. Your device turns
  it into a scrambled code, and we keep only a further scrambled version of that code, which can't
  be turned back into your password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account
  identifier, email address and name from Google.
- **What you keep in the app**, so it can be synced between your devices:
  - your daily routine (wake-up, meal, coffee, exercise and bedtime times), time zone, language
    and colour theme;
  - your stack: the New Roots Herbal products you take, how often, the times you chose, and
    suggestions you dismissed;
  - products from other brands, **medications** and foods you add yourself, with the details you
    enter (brand, name, barcode, NPN or DIN, strength, format, dose, ingredients, label directions,
    warnings and notes);
  - how many pills or servings are left in each bottle, and your shopping list;
  - which doses you ticked as taken, for the last 3 days only.
- **Technical information**: the devices signed in to your account and when your account was last
  used.

Your list of supplements and medications is health-related information. We treat it as
sensitive and store it only with your express consent, given when you create your account.

## 3. Your health profile (optional)

With an account, you can fill in a health profile: year of birth, gender, pregnancy or
breastfeeding, health conditions (for example high blood pressure or IBS), health goals, diet,
foods you avoid and activity level. Every question is optional and has a "prefer not to say"
answer.

We store your health profile only after you consent to it, separately from your account. It
**never changes your schedule** and isn't medical advice. You can change it or delete it at any
time without deleting your account.

## 4. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

- build your daily schedule, remind you to take your products and keep your devices in sync;
- track your bottles, build your shopping list and, when a product from another brand is running
  out, suggest a comparable New Roots Herbal product;
- fill in the details of a product from Health Canada's public databases when you ask (we send
  Health Canada only the NPN or DIN, never anything about you);
- send you account emails (confirming your email address, resetting your password, telling you
  your password changed, and warning you before an unused account is deleted);
- send you news notifications, if you turn them on (section 7);
- protect the service (for example, limiting repeated failed sign-ins);
- improve our catalogue: we count, without any account identifier, which other-brand products
  people add (brand, name and barcode), to decide which products to add and which New Roots
  Herbal alternatives to suggest.

## 5. Automated processing and profiling

Your schedule is calculated automatically from your routine and the timing rules attached to each
product (for example, keeping iron away from calcium). It's a suggestion: you can move any product
to another time. On request, we will explain how a placement was decided; the app also shows the
reason for each placement under "More info".

Using your health profile to choose which news you receive is **profiling**. It is **off** unless
you turn on "Use my health profile to send me relevant news", and you can turn it off at any time.

## 6. Consent and how to withdraw it

We ask for your consent when you create an account, before storing a health profile, before
using your health profile for news, and before sending news notifications. Anything optional is
off until you turn it on. You can withdraw a consent at any time in the app (switch it off, delete
your health profile or delete your account) or by contacting our Privacy Officer.

## 7. News notifications

If you turn on "New products, webinars and offers" in the Notifications screen, New Roots Herbal
may send notifications about new products, webinars and offers to that device, at most one a day.
Each one identifies New Roots Herbal. You can turn them off at any time in the Notifications
screen (on Android, also from the notification itself). If you also turned on the health-profile
option, the news you receive may be chosen according to your profile; staff who prepare news only
ever see how many devices a message will reach, never who you are.

## 8. Notifications on your lock screen

Notifications can appear on your lock screen. By default, reminders don't name your products
("Time for 3 products — 9:30 AM"). You can choose to show product names in the Notifications
screen.

## 9. Who processes your information, and where

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We use these
service providers, under written agreements, only to run SmartStack:

| Provider | What it does for SmartStack | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Hosts the app and its database; delivers reminders | Cloudflare data centres; the database is in Eastern North America, which may be the United States |
| SMTP2GO | Sends account emails (your email address and the message) | [To confirm: the data region of our SMTP2GO account, e.g. the United States] |
| Google LLC | Sign-in, only if you choose "Continue with Google" | Google's data centres, under Google's own privacy policy |
| Your browser's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla) | Carries notifications to your device; the content is encrypted and can't be read by them | Their data centres |

Your information may therefore be stored or processed **outside Quebec and outside Canada**,
including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local laws.
Before doing so, we assessed the risks and put protections in place (contracts, encryption,
keeping only what's needed).

Inside New Roots Herbal, only the few people who operate SmartStack can access the database, and
only when necessary to run or fix the service.

## 10. How long we keep your information

| Information | Kept |
| --- | --- |
| Account, stack, products, bottles, shopping list | Until you delete them or your account; accounts unused for 3 years are deleted, after an email warning 30 days before |
| Health profile | Until you delete it or your account |
| Ticked doses | 3 days |
| Deleted items (for syncing your devices) | 30 days |
| Sign-in sessions | Until you log out, or 90 days without use |
| Email links (confirmation, password reset) | Until used or expired (1 to 48 hours), then deleted within a day |
| Reminders | 7 days after their time |
| Device information without an account | Until "Delete my data", or 12 months without use |
| Consent records | As long as the account or device they belong to |
| Database backups (Cloudflare) | Deleted information disappears from backups within 7 days |
| Service logs (Cloudflare) | 3 days |

## 11. How we protect your information

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The database is encrypted at rest. Passwords never leave your
device and can't be recovered from what we store. Sign-in codes and email links are stored only in
scrambled form. Repeated failed sign-ins are limited. Our logs don't record the content of your
stack or your health profile. Access to the systems is limited to the people who operate them.

## 12. Your rights

You can:

- **see** your information: everything is visible in the app, and "Download my data" gives you a
  copy in a structured, commonly used format (JSON);
- **correct** it: edit it in the app;
- **delete** it: delete a product, your health profile or your whole account in the app, or "Delete
  my data" without an account;
- **withdraw your consent** (section 6);
- **ask how an automated placement was decided** (section 5);
- **ask any question or make a request** to our Privacy Officer (section 16). We answer within
  30 days.

## 13. Children

SmartStack is for people 14 years of age or older. We don't knowingly collect information from
children under 14. If you believe a child under 14 has created an account, contact our Privacy
Officer and we will delete it.

## 14. Cookies and similar technologies

SmartStack doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It saves your information in
your device's storage so the app works offline. Links to newrootsherbal.com (for example "Buy
online" or a news link) include tags that tell the website the visit came from SmartStack; the
website's own privacy policy then applies.

## 15. Changes to this policy

When we make an important change, we will tell you in the app and, where the law requires it, ask
for your consent again before the change applies to you. The version and date are at the top of
this page.
